About Us

Dedicated to Animal Welfare in Cagayan de Oro

The City Veterinary Office (City Vet CDO) is committed to promoting responsible pet ownership, eradicating rabies, and safeguarding animal and public health across all barangays. Our digitized portal ensures every Kagay-anon has streamlined access to essential veterinary services, including spaying, neutering, consultations, and community vaccination drives.

Public Guidelines

Operating Hours

Walk-ins and surgeries are processed from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday.

Appointment Required

To secure a slot for Spay & Neuter procedures, please create an account and register your pet's profile.

Barangay Drives

Free Anti-Rabies vaccinations are conducted directly in barangays. Check the calendar for your area's schedule.
